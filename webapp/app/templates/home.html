The public-only guarantee

The full Methodex corpus mixes public-domain US-federal material with in-copyright international / academic sources. This hosted instance runs with METHODEX_PUBLIC_ONLY=1: the in-memory stores are filtered once at load to public-domain documents and their events, so every page, API call, and download serves only the public-domain layer. Legal basis: 17 U.S.C. §105.
