About Methodex

Methodex is a "Context7 for economic methodology": it collects, transcribes, structures, and serves the construction-and-revision history of every economic statistic, cross-linked to the data vintage. The killer query — "How was U.S. GDP measured in 1985 vs 2015, and what changed?" — is answered with citations to the governing manual edition in force at each date.

The public-only guarantee

The full Methodex corpus mixes public-domain US-federal-government material (servable verbatim) with in-copyright international and academic sources (digest-only). This hosted instance runs with METHODEX_PUBLIC_ONLY=1. The canonical in-memory stores are filtered once at load to public-domain documents and the events, measures, and statistics derived from them — so every page, API response, and download serves only the public-domain layer. No in-copyright, international, academic, or unknown-producer material can appear anywhere on this site. Legal basis: 17 U.S.C. §105 (works of the U.S. federal government are not subject to copyright).

Privacy & telemetry

This site emits privacy-first usage telemetry conforming to the Carson Telemetry Standard v1.0: one event per request with a daily-salted, truncated hash of the client IP — no raw IP, no PII, no cookies, no consent banner. Aggregate counts answer "what is used, how often"; they cannot track a person across days or sites.

Methodology & the MCP

The same data also powers the Methodex MCP server (get_methodology(statistic, as_of_date) and 12 sibling tools), so an LLM can answer methodology questions with provenance. This website is the public, browsable face of that corpus.
